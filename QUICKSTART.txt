NEXTSET UA — YOUR TELEGRAM TRAINING DIARY
========================================

1. Extract the entire ZIP into Documents or another writable folder.
2. Double-click NextSet.exe. No Python installation is needed.
3. First run only: paste your bot token from https://t.me/BotFather.
   The input is hidden. Keep the token private.
4. Open the pairing link shown in the window and press Start in Telegram.
5. Send: Жим лежачи 3x10 60 кг

History: /history
Chart: /graph 30 Жим лежачи
Help: /help

Leave the app open and the PC awake. Next time just open NextSet.exe.
Stop: Ctrl+C or double-click STOP.cmd.
Do not run the same bot token on two computers at once.

BACKUP / MOVE TO ANOTHER PC
The app creates one local backup per day in data/backups (latest 14).
/backup creates another snapshot. To restore, stop the bot and open
RESTORE.cmd. Choose a backup and type RESTORE; the current database is
backed up before replacement. Archives contain your diary, not your token.
For a new PC, copy an archive into data/backups, restore, then start the
app and enter your token if needed. Keep the old instance stopped.

Stop the app, then copy the data folder. It contains private credentials
and your diary. Place it beside NextSet.exe on your home PC before starting.
For an update, replace the program files and keep your data folder.

OPTIONAL LOCAL AI
The app works without AI. Double-click SETUP_AI.cmd once.
If Ollama is missing, install it from the official page that opens, then
run SETUP_AI.cmd again to download the model. Later bot starts also start
the local AI service. Use /ai and review every proposal before /confirm.

TRAINING PLANS
In Telegram: /programs, then /coachhelp for profile and PR setup.
Five orientations for experienced lifters; Qwen generates individual
proposals from local evidence. AI setup is required for coaching.
/block 4 or /block 6 proposes a recurring block; /blockconfirm accepts.
/checkin records sleep, energy, soreness and available time with buttons.
/planconfirm accepts the next session; log actual work, then /done.
/feedback records exercise effort and pain; /swap proposes an equipment
alternative, accepted with /swapconfirm (then /planconfirm).
/weekly shows your week; /weeklyai adds source-grounded interpretation.
/weeklyremind on enables a Sunday report while the PC is awake.
/status for illness or fatigue, /extra for a light fourth day.
EVALUATE_AI.cmd checks six synthetic cases against your real local model.
Coaching is experimental. App checks do not prove scientific correctness.
